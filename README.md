## Building systems that turn learning evidence into action

I founded **Foundations to Mastery** to solve a problem I kept seeing in education: capable students carrying hidden gaps for years, then mistaking the consequences for personal inability.

The goal is not to produce another score, dashboard, or generic study plan. It is to locate where understanding first became unstable, show the evidence behind that conclusion, and build a clear path forward.

My work brings together **diagnostic reasoning, curriculum design, learning systems, and full-stack product engineering**. I design the logic, content, data structures, educator workflows, and software that connect a learner's first intake to individualized instruction and measurable progress.

> A learner should never be reduced to a grade-level label. We should be able to show what is secure, where the foundation begins to break, and what to teach next.

## What I am building

| System | What it is designed to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Diagnostic systems** | Gather structured evidence across standards, representations, and response types without reducing a learner to one test score. |
| **Foundation maps** | Connect skills to their prerequisite structure so a visible mistake can be traced to the earliest point that needs repair. |
| **Personalized learning paths** | Turn diagnostic evidence into sequenced instruction, practice, reassessment, and a realistic route back to mastery. |
| **Tutor and family tools** | Give educators clear next actions while giving families understandable reports, progress, materials, and program scope. |
| **Curriculum infrastructure** | Produce structured math, computer science, and science materials through reproducible authoring, publishing, and quality-assurance workflows. |
| **Human-accountable AI** | Use models to assist with grading, synthesis, and drafting while preserving educator review, traceability, and responsibility. |

## From evidence to action

<p align="center">
  <strong>Intake → Diagnose → Map the foundation → Build the path → Teach → Re-check</strong>
</p>

Every stage should answer a real question:

- **What does the learner understand?**
- **Where does that understanding stop being reliable?**
- **What sits underneath the difficulty?**
- **What should an educator do next?**
- **What new evidence will show that the foundation is stronger?**

The point is not a prettier report. The point is to give every conclusion a trace and every trace a next action.

## How I build

- **Evidence before labels.** A conclusion should be tied to what the learner actually did, not assumed from age, course, or grade.
- **Systems before one-off artifacts.** Curriculum, diagnostics, reports, and workflows should remain useful as the program grows.
- **Educator judgment where it matters.** Automation can remove repetitive work without removing human accountability.
- **Honest boundaries.** Confidence, limitations, provenance, and unresolved questions should stay visible.
- **Privacy by design.** Learner information belongs behind deliberate access controls, not inside public demos or portfolio claims.
